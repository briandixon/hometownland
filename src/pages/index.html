Questions

What landowners ask

What does this cost me?

Nothing. We pay for the research and the title work, and there is no fee for receiving an offer.

Am I obligated once I submit the form?

No. Submitting the form starts a conversation and nothing more. You are free to accept our offer, counter it, or walk away.

How do you arrive at your number?

We look at recent comparable sales in the same county and acreage range, then adjust for legal access, zoning and permitted use, topography, and the condition of the title. We are happy to walk you through the reasoning.

What if I do not know the parcel number?

Send whatever you have — an address, a nearby cross street, or a description. We can usually locate the parcel in the county records ourselves.
